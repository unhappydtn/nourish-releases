# Nourish

Plan a week of food and watch every nutrient fill up: protein, vitamins, minerals and more,
measured against goals made for you (age, body, activity, and your own goals such as losing fat or a health focus).
For **Mac** and **Windows**: the same app, the same food database, the same version.

- 13,500+ foods from USDA FoodData Central, all included in the app: no account, works offline
- Daily bars for 35+ nutrients, with safe upper limits
- Food suggestions for what you're low on
- Packaged foods: photograph the nutrition label (Vietnamese, English, Japanese, Korean, Thai, Chinese and more) or scan the barcode
- Grocery list by aisle, saved lists, supplements, light and dark mode
- Updates itself

## Install

### Mac

> ⚠️ The current .dmg isn't working yet. A fix is on the way.

1. Open **Nourish.dmg** and drag **Nourish** into **Applications**.
2. Open Nourish from Applications. The first time, macOS says it can't verify the developer, because Nourish
   isn't signed with a paid Apple developer certificate yet. Click **Done** (not Move to Trash).
3. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down to the message about Nourish and click **Open Anyway**,
   then confirm with your password or Touch ID.
4. From then on, Nourish opens normally.

### Windows

1. Run **Nourish-Setup.exe**.
2. Windows may show **"Windows protected your PC"**, because Nourish isn't signed with a paid code-signing
   certificate yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Nourish installs for your user account (no administrator password needed) and opens. It's in the Start menu
   from then on.
4. Label photos are read on your PC by readers built into Nourish (nothing is uploaded). To scan with the webcam,
   allow camera access in **Settings → Privacy & security → Camera**.

## Updates

New versions arrive inside the app on both Mac and Windows: you'll see an **Update & restart** banner. You don't
need to download anything again.

## Your data

Your profile, plans and lists stay on your computer. Nothing is uploaded. When you scan a barcode, only that
number is looked up in Open Food Facts.

- Mac: `~/Library/Application Support/com.nourish.desktop`
- Windows: `%APPDATA%\com.nourish.desktop`

To uninstall: on Mac, drag Nourish from Applications to the Trash; on Windows, **Settings → Apps → Installed apps
→ Nourish → Uninstall**. Your data folder stays unless you delete it.

## Good to know

  guidance, not medical advice.
