# Nourish

Plan a week of food on your Mac and watch every nutrient fill up: protein, vitamins, minerals and more,
measured against goals made for you (age, body, activity, and your own goals such as losing fat or a health focus).

- 13,500+ foods from USDA FoodData Central, all included in the app: no account, works offline
- Daily bars for 35+ nutrients, with safe upper limits
- Food suggestions for what you're low on
- Packaged foods: photograph the nutrition label (Vietnamese, English, Japanese, Korean, Thai, Chinese and more) or scan the barcode
- Grocery list by aisle, saved lists, supplements, light and dark mode
- Updates itself

## Download

**[⬇ Download Nourish for Mac](https://github.com/unhappydtn/nourish-releases/releases/latest/download/Nourish.dmg)**

Needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 13 Ventura or later.

## Install

1. Open **Nourish.dmg** and drag **Nourish** into **Applications**.
2. Open Nourish from Applications. The first time, macOS says it can't verify the developer, because Nourish
   isn't signed with a paid Apple developer certificate yet. Click **Done** (not Move to Trash).
3. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down to the message about Nourish and click **Open Anyway**,
   then confirm with your password or Touch ID.
4. From then on, Nourish opens normally. New versions arrive inside the app: you'll see an
   **Update & restart** banner.

Your data (profile, plans, lists) stays on your Mac in `~/Library/Application Support/com.nourish.desktop`.
Nothing is uploaded. When you scan a barcode, only that number is looked up in Open Food Facts.

## Good to know

- Goals follow the US/Canada Dietary Reference Intakes and published guidance for healthy adults. Nourish is
  guidance, not medical advice.
- This repository holds the released app only. Every version and its notes are under
  [Releases](https://github.com/unhappydtn/nourish-releases/releases).
